01

Question

  • The attenuation map sets the per-pixel strength of the watermark residual
  • Does a learned map beat the fixed JND map on the quality–robustness trade-off?
  • Does the gain survive when distortion (PSNR), not the embedding coefficient, is held fixed?
02

Method

  • Pixel Seal embedder and extractor frozen; a U-Net predicts the attenuation map
  • SAA (Shape-Aligned Attenuation): aligns the mean-normalized learned map with the JND map
  • Optional LPIPS perceptual loss
  • Attack: combined JPEG + crop + brightness
  • Comparison at matched coefficient, matched PSNR, and matched robustness
  • SA-1B (n = 1,000) · 3 seeds · paired bootstrap CIs
03

Findings

ComparisonResult
Same coefficientBase learned map: bit accuracy +3.81 pp, PSNR −5.37 dB
+ LPIPS + SAARecovers +2.05 dB at a 0.31 pp accuracy cost
Matched PSNRAdvantage disappears; fixed JND leads by 0.36–0.38 pp (all but the highest target)
Among learned mapsRobustness differences come from SAA (+0.55–2.59 pp)
Matched robustnessLearned maps' LPIPS edge largely vanishes; fixed JND needs the least distortion
MaskWM · WAM · VideoSealMatched PSNR narrows the JPEG gap to < 1.2 pp (learned slightly ahead on VideoSeal)
Takeaway. With the backbone frozen, learned attenuation beats fixed JND neither in robustness at equal distortion nor clearly in quality at equal robustness — single-coefficient comparisons overstate its benefit.
